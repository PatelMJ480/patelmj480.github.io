About TubeAssistant

TubeAssistant is a personal software project developed to simplify parts of the YouTube content creation process using artificial intelligence and automation.

The system can help prepare video scripts, narration, visual assets, thumbnails and publishing metadata.

Project capabilities

AI Content Creation

Generate video topics, scripts, descriptions and relevant metadata.

Video Preparation

Prepare narration, visual assets, captions and thumbnails.

Human Approval

Require an explicit human decision before a video can proceed to YouTube publishing.

Human approval is mandatory

TubeAssistant is designed so that a video cannot be published through the approval workflow without an explicit human approval decision.
