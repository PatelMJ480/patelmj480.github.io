Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

1. Introduction

TubeAssistant is a personal software project developed by PatelMJ480. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when a user chooses to use the application's YouTube integration and related automation features.

2. Information accessed

When a user connects a Google or YouTube account and grants permission, TubeAssistant may access information permitted by the selected Google OAuth scopes, including:

  • YouTube account and channel information.
  • YouTube video information and publishing-related data.
  • Channel analytics information when the relevant permission is used.
  • Video metadata, captions and other information required by enabled features.
  • OAuth authorization information required to maintain the connection.

Information is accessed only for the features the user chooses to use and the permissions granted by the user.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • Review channel performance and assist with content strategy.
  • Generate video titles, descriptions, tags and related content.
  • Prepare and manage video uploads when that feature is enabled.
  • Maintain video approval records and publishing history.
  • Provide requested automation and operational functionality.

4. Human approval

The TubeAssistant publishing workflow requires explicit human approval before a video can proceed to YouTube publishing. A rejected or unapproved video must not be published through this workflow.

5. Third-party services

Depending on the features enabled, TubeAssistant may interact with these services:

  • Google and YouTube: account authorization and YouTube API operations.
  • GitHub: source-code hosting and automated workflow execution.
  • Cloudflare: cloud processing, database records and temporary asset storage.
  • Telegram: notifications, video previews and human approval controls.
  • AI and media providers: processing content-generation prompts or media requests when enabled.

These providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Storage and security

Application information may be stored in local project files, protected configuration files, approved cloud secret stores, databases and cloud object storage, depending on the active deployment.

Reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect authorization credentials and project information. Passwords, OAuth tokens and client secrets should never be shared publicly or committed to source-code repositories.

7. Data sharing

TubeAssistant does not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to the third-party providers necessary to operate features explicitly requested by the user.

8. Retention and deletion

Information is retained only as needed for application operation, approval records, security and troubleshooting. Retention periods may depend on the storage provider and enabled workflow.

Users may revoke TubeAssistant's Google account access through their Google Account permissions and may request assistance with application-stored information by contacting the developer.

9. Cookies and website analytics

This informational website does not currently use custom tracking cookies, advertising pixels or website analytics services.

10. Children's privacy

TubeAssistant is not intended to collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when application features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
